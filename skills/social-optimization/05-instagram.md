# Instagram optimization

Usually lower priority than LinkedIn/X/GitHub for a professional/technical presence — treat as optional polish unless the person specifically wants it prioritized (e.g. they run a visible content/photography side-practice, or Instagram is where they get inbound client leads).

## Do first
1. Bio — same self-label discipline as everywhere else. Watch specifically for hype-y self-titles ("10x Engineer", etc.) that contradict the calibration established in Phase 0 — Instagram bios are a common place for this kind of drift since they're often set once, early, and never revisited.
2. Name field vs. handle — a name field stuffed with a slogan ("Name | 10x Whatever") reads differently than a clean name; recommend simplifying unless the person is deliberately running it as a personal-brand/business account.
3. Link in bio — should point to the current, live portfolio/contact surface.

## What NOT to do
- Don't recommend a new profile photo or full rebrand unless asked — if the person says they'll handle visuals themselves, respect that and just fix text fields.
- Don't assume the same professional-only tone as LinkedIn is correct here by default — Instagram often intentionally carries more personal/creative content (e.g. a photography hobby) and that can coexist with a professional bio line rather than being replaced by it.
